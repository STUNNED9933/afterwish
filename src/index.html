Afterwish

What falls here does not stay down.

Whatever you cut down on the road above Haven gets back up. It always has. The people of Haven stopped asking why a long time ago, and the ones who know will not say. Parry at the flash, climb, and find out what keeps them walking. Alone, or with friends.

Co-op exploration

Play with friends

One of you hosts and gets a room code. Everyone else types the code to join (up to 4 players). You explore the same map together; each of you keeps your own character, loot and quest progress, and it saves on your own computer.

Host

Join

Friends join through a free matchmaking service and then connect straight to the host. Some school or work networks block this; home internet and phone hotspots usually work.

Your saves

Move to another browser

Saves live in the browser you play in. To carry them over: export here, then import in the other browser. Every character, the shared gold and shards, and your settings come along.
