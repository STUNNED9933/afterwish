The story so fara minute's read

The tear. You woke in a glade in the Wakewood, with no idea how you got there: only a crack in the air where you came through, and a name that is nearly yours. Small, weak, and very far from home.

The Iron Hounds. Sorrel, a scholar who had come to study the crack, found you there instead. His company took you in: Rowan, who leads them and swings a sword bigger than you; Juni, the archer who never stops talking; Ilse, who carries the shield; and Sorrel. They asked all over Sunmere for your family. Nobody came.

The Telling Glass. Days with the Hounds got your strength back. At the Adventurers' Guild the Telling Glass judged you: an aptitude of three, and no element at all. Rank F, says Magistra Odile, and rank F does not change. Then the glass cracked under someone else's hands, and nobody could say why.

Now. The Hounds don't care what the glass says. They'll train you anyway: Rowan's way, or Juni's.

You keep the experience the skipped quests would have given you. The story goes on after you choose (it is in your journal, J, as Main Story, and you can leave it for as long as you like), and you can play the opening any time from the corner.
