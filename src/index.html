    Controls

    Right-click
    Move. Right-click an enemy to attack it.
    A or Shift + Click
    Attack-move: attack the nearest enemy (to you or your cursor: set it in the pause menu), or walk there and attack the first one you meet.
    Space
    Parry. Press it when an enemy flashes white.
    Q W E R
    Abilities, aimed at your cursor.
    Alt, Ctrl or Shift + Q
    Spend a skill point to rank that ability up.
    1
    Drink a health potion.
    D F
    Borrowed skills, once another class has beaten the Quiet (choose them in Talents).
    Tab
    Gear and stats.
    Esc
    Pause, settings, save and quit.
    Right-click
    On a person or a glimmer (a floating sliver of starlight): walk over and talk, or see what it remembers. G does the same when you are next to one. Right-click outside a window to close it.
    Pale gold attack: parry it Red attack: get out of the way
